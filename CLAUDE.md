The purpose of this repo is to gather together a curated list of projects exploring the use of AI for decision making support and evaluation.

This includes projects using LLMs, agentic AI, and analytical frameworks for decision support systems, decision quality evaluation, scenario analysis, and structured decision-making assistance.

When organising the information into README.md, adhere to these preferences:

- Organise by section
- Use shields.io badges for GitHub repos with star count and last commit
- Short descriptions copied from projects
- Author notes in italics to distinguish from project descriptions
- Separate sections for websites and demos/POCs
